## Writing principle

Separate **what has been implemented**, **what has been experimentally observed**, **what is theoretically established by prior work**, and **what remains an untested proposal**. The strongest Bachelor thesis is not the one with the largest novelty claim; it is the one whose methods, assumptions, and results survive close examination.
